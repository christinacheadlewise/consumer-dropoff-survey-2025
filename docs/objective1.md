# Research Objective 1: Why Customers Don't Convert

> Identify and quantify the top reasons why consumer customers do not convert on any products in the first 30 days.

## Top Non-Conversion Factors (Ranked by Evidence)

### Factor 1: Timing Mismatch (largest group)

~54% of drop-offs simply haven't needed the service yet. They signed up in advance of travel or a transfer and are waiting for the triggering event.

- 82% of drop-offs said "Not yet, but I plan to"
- Open-end themes: "haven't travelled yet" (23%), "no need yet / waiting" (33%)
- **These are NOT lost customers** — they're pre-registered and intend to convert
- Behavioural validation: 81% of "not yet" respondents have actually adopted per Snowflake data

### Factor 2: Invest/Assets Product Gap (highest drop-off rate)

| Product | Drop-off rate |
|---|---|
| Card | 10.1% |
| Send | 9.3% |
| Receive | 8.1% |
| **Invest** | **46.7%** |
| Other | 18.5% |

- Chi-square: p<0.0001 (strongest predictor of drop-off)
- Likely a product readiness or feature gap, not an onboarding issue

### Factor 3: Signup Friction for Receive-Money Users

Receive-money drop-offs rated signup ease significantly lower than converted peers:
- Drop-off mean: 3.62/5
- Converted mean: 4.41/5
- This is the largest ease gap of any task type

Suggests the verification flow is harder for people whose first action is receiving.

### Factor 4: Uncertainty About Use Case

| Frequency intent | Drop-off | Converted |
|---|---|---|
| One time | 22.4% | 19.9% |
| Regular | 49.0% | 63.2% |
| **Not sure yet** | **28.6%** | **16.8%** |

Drop-offs are 1.7x more likely to be "not sure" about frequency (p=0.08, directional). They lack a clear, recurring use case.

**⚠ Note:** Team flagged that "regularly" may be interpreted retrospectively. See [Correlations](correlations.md) for discussion.

### Factor 5: Information Needs

| Help needed? | Drop-off | Converted |
|---|---|---|
| No, it was easy | 61.9% | 78.2% |
| Other (unspecified) | 17.0% | 2.4% |

Only 62% of drop-offs found the process easy (vs 78% converted). The 17% citing "Other" help sources may indicate unmet information needs.

### Factor 6: Card Delivery / Physical Access

Qualitative evidence from open-ends:
- Cards lost in mail
- Ordered too close to travel
- Using digital card while waiting for physical

### Factor 7: Verification Barriers

From Q5 themes (what was difficult):
- ID/passport issues
- Proof of address difficulty
- Account pauses for document checks

27% of difficulty reports mention verification — the #1 cited friction theme.

## What About Competitors?

Only 5 of 26 who truly didn't convert went to another provider. Reasons:
- Service not available in country (1)
- No additional benefits (1)
- Other (3)

**Competitors are not the primary threat.** Non-conversion is overwhelmingly about timing and product fit, not competitive loss.

## LTV Impact

| Group | Median Revenue | n |
|---|---|---|
| Converted | £28.27 | 1,184 |
| Drop-off | £12.16 | 147 |

But 88% of "drop-offs" have generated SOME revenue — most eventually found a different path to value.
